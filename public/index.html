Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work? +
Start with our free plan - no credit card required. You get 3 projects and 50 tasks to explore all features. Upgrade to Pro when you're ready for unlimited access.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes! Cancel your subscription anytime from your account settings. You'll continue to have Pro access until the end of your billing period, then automatically switch to the free plan.
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely. We use bank-grade encryption, regular security audits, and are SOC 2 compliant. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never share it with third parties.
Do you offer team discounts? +
Yes! Teams of 10+ users get 20% off. Contact our sales team for custom enterprise pricing and features like SSO, advanced permissions, and dedicated support.
What integrations are available? +
We integrate with 50+ tools including Google Calendar, Slack, GitHub, Zapier, and more. Pro users also get access to our REST API for custom integrations.
